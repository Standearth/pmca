We have a meeting with Amazon on May 20th! Share your questions.

I'm OG Strogatz. I live in California, I'm a longtime Prime member, and last fall I joined the Leadership Team of Prime Members for a Cleaner Amazon.

I am excited to share that Amazon has agreed to sit down with us again!

On May 20th, a few of us from the Prime Members for a Cleaner Amazon Leadership Team will meet with a senior member of Amazon's sustainability team — a step up from our first meeting earlier this year. That doesn't happen because a company feels like chatting. It happens because more than 31,000 Prime members signed on and made it impossible to ignore.

We want to bring your perspectives into the conversation. What would you ask Amazon if you were in the room?

Take one minute to send us your question.

We'll raise the strongest questions in the meeting on May 20th and report back on what we hear.

Over a million people have now visited our website and the word about our campaign is spreading around the world. We now have thousands of Prime members joining from India, UK and from across the EU. Amazon knows that they can’t ignore our demands any longer.

Thank you for being in this with us.

OG Strogatz PMCA Leadership Team
