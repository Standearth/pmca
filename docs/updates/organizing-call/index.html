Our first organizing call was INCREDIBLE!

I am so excited to share that there are now more than 20,000 Prime members calling for a cleaner Amazon! 🥳 Last week, we held the first ever mobilizing call, and the turnout was amazing. Thank you for being a part of this movement.

As a follow-up to this incredible call, I want to share with you this inspiring video highlighting Prime Members that have added their voices to this conversation. They had such inspiring things to share about why they care about Amazon’s impact on climate and communities – and about what they want to do to address it.

WATCH AND SHARE THIS INCREDIBLE VIDEO OF FELLOW PRIME MEMBERS CALLING OUT AMAZON

To move Amazon, we’ve got a lot more work ahead of us. Holiday shopping season is right around the corner, and we plan to use it to highlight the challenge Amazon poses to the people and places we love. In just a bit, we’ll reach out with some specific ways that you can remain engaged with this movement.

In the meantime, I have one small favour to ask. After you’ve checked out our highlights reel from the mobilizing call, I want you to text five friends and ask them to join Prime Members for a Cleaner Amazon. The most powerful thing we can do right now to get Amazon’s attention is to show that our numbers continue growing.

Thank you again for being a part of this growing community. In these times of political impotence, holding corporations accountable is one of the most impactful actions we can take on climate. Whoever holds the power in Washington, corporations will always worry the most about their bottom line. And as Amazon’s most valuable customers, we have a unique power to hold the company accountable.
