We took climate accountability to Amazon’s front door

When one of the world’s largest corporations drags its feet on climate action, its customers have no choice but to deliver a clear message to its doorstep. Earlier this month, your generous donation helped us do exactly that at Amazon’s headquarters in Seattle, WA.

Thanks to you, our team was able to greet Amazon employees arriving to work with a clear message: our movement is growing and we are not backing down. Your support helped distribute hundreds of Prime Members for Cleaner Amazon (PMCA) flyers and logo stickers, engaging hundreds of Amazon employees and Seattle residents in the process.

And because you and your fellow Stand.earth community members helped fund four mobile billboards, we were able to cover multiple entrances around Amazon’s HQ all at once. We brought our message to the main entrances for both cars and commuters, and onto the iconic Amazon campus lawn (see photos) and finished strong with a final photo shoot at the historic Pike Place Market.

This action delivered exactly what we set out to do: visible, unavoidable pressure at Amazon’s front door, and the implied suggestion that this is just the beginning. Over the course of an hour, we engaged hundreds of arriving employees and passersby, distributing 300 PMCA flyers and logo stickers before moving into a series of highly visible photoshoots across the one-block HQ campus. When the photoshoot drew Amazon’s private security out in force, our trained police liaison skillfully kept them at bay, ensuring the action continued smoothly.

Friend, actions like this increase internal pressure on Amazon’s leadership by showing that customers are demanding climate accountability. And guess what? Our message didn’t just end there – it was picked up by Common Dreams and Europe Says.

Thank you again for making this bold and effective action possible. This action was one step in our campaign to hold Amazon accountable. Stay tuned, there is more to come!

In the meantime, if you’d like to help this movement gain even more momentum, you can take a simple step right now: recruit ONE Amazon Prime member to join Prime Members for a Cleaner Amazon.
