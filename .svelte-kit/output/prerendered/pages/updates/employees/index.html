Amazon's other big problem

Did you know that since Jeff Bezos announced Amazon’s Climate Pledge, the company’s emissions have gone up by at least 35%? We already know the emissions costs of sprawling warehouses, diesel powered trucks and omnipresent delivery vans congesting our neighborhoods. And rather than cleaning up its flawed logistics chain, Amazon is ramping up its emissions by relentlessly pursuing AI.

There’s no way around it. Amazon’s AI Data center buildout is undermining its own climate progress. Data centers are usually powered by fossil fuels—including coal plants that had been scheduled to close. This is bad.

As Bloomberg reported in 2024, without the dubious math of Renewable Energy Credits to fudge the numbers, Amazon would be “forced to admit that its 2022 emissions [were]... three times what the company disclosed.”

You read that right: emissions are three times what Amazon previously disclosed. And that was at the very start of its $100 billion, 5-year data center expansion.

Amazon Employees for Climate Justice (AECJ), who spurred the Climate Pledge, have just released an Open Letter on AI that names disastrous impacts of Amazon’s stubborn AI buildout. AECJ is rallying both Amazon employees and the general public to this cause.

  • If you’re an Amazon employee, you can sign the letter here.
  • If you’re not an Amazon employee, sign the solidarity letter here and share with your networks – particularly anyone in the tech industry.

There are plenty of other reasons to be alarmed about this dogged AI trajectory. Neighborhoods adjacent to data centers experiencing service disruption and rising prices. Aquifers drained to cool data centers. Communities surveilled by AI powered tools deployed by increasingly authoritarian governments.

Now, more than ever, we must remind Amazon that it cannot pursue profits at all costs. Our communities cannot accept a future where our health, our natural world and our future is jeopardized by a merciless trillion dollar company. Let’s remind Amazon of its climate promises.

The Stand.earth community is no stranger to taking on big targets (and winning). We’ve worked to protect millions of acres of forests from extractive industries. We’ve gone up against multinational oil, gas, and coal companies worth billions of dollars and come out victorious, blocking dozens of their projects that would have poisoned local communities and accelerated the climate crisis. It’s time to remind Amazon what community power looks like.
