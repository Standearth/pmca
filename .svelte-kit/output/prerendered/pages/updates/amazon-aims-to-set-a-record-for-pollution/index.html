Amazon Aims to Set a Record: For Pollution

By Bill Pevec, MD. PMCA Leadership Team

Amazon promotes itself as a leader in environmental sustainability. In 2019, Amazon co-founded the Climate Pledge, vowing to reach net-zero carbon emissions by 2040. However, according to Amazon’s own 2025 sustainability report, its emissions increased by 16% from 2024 to 2025 — and they have risen every year for several years running. That is the opposite direction of net zero.

Asked about that record, an Amazon spokeswoman told the New York Times that “the world looks different now than when we co-founded the climate pledge.” Still, she said, “our commitment hasn’t changed.”

But words are cheap. In truth, Amazon is positioning itself to be a leader in environmental pollution.

And now, Amazon is backsliding even further. The company has bought a site in Pecos County, Texas, where it is investing in a gas-burning power plant to feed a new data center — a plant permitted to release up to 33 million tons of carbon dioxide per year. That permit gives it room to more than double the emissions of the nation’s most polluting power plant, the coal-burning James H. Miller Jr. Power Plant in Alabama, which emits about 16 million tons of carbon dioxide per year.

The scale of the new plant is difficult to comprehend. It would be built around 35 gas turbines capable of producing up to 7.65 gigawatts — enough electricity to power millions of homes — and every watt of it would be dedicated to a single company’s data center. At first it would not connect to the public grid at all.

And the damage would not only be measured in carbon. Like coal plants, gas plants throw off smog-forming pollutants linked to asthma and heart disease. Environmental and public health groups have warned that a plant this size would fall hardest on the air, and the people, of West Texas.

And Amazon would not be the last. The problem is not that Amazon wants to make its own electricity — it is what Amazon plans to burn to make it. If it gets away with this, more plants like this one become the default for every tech giant chasing the same boom.

It is time that consumers hold accountable the corporations that are destroying our ability to live and be healthy. If you agree, don’t stand idly by while this corporation mortgages our collective future for its own economic gain.

Prime Members for a Cleaner Amazon has three demands of Amazon:

  • Electrify your global delivery fleet from dock to door
  • Power every data center and warehouse with real renewable energy, not new fossil fuel plants
  • Create better working conditions, including fair break times, safety standards, and protecting workers’ right to organize
