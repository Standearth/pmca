PMCA update for July 2026

We’re Amazon Prime members, and we joined Prime Members for a Cleaner Amazon (PMCA) because we want the convenience of Amazon without the cost to our climate and communities.

Here’s an update on the campaign and a breakdown of the company’s new sustainability report:

---------------------------

Prime Members for a Cleaner Amazon (PMCA) launched in the fall of 2025 and has grown to over 30,000 members around the world. ✅ Amazing, right? And…Amazon is HUGE, and growing, and Amazon continues to pollute. Every day. 👎

That’s why it’s crucial we continue to grow this campaign. PMCA is building power to motivate Amazon to change…to move away from fossil fuel, achieve 100% zero emissions last mile delivery by 2030, treat its workers and communities with dignity and respect, to respect human rights, and power its data centers and warehouses with renewable energy. 💪

What Amazon's new sustainability report tells us (and what it doesn't):

Amazon released its annual sustainability report this month, and we want to be fair about it. There's progress. The company now has over 52,000 electric delivery vehicles on the road globally, with over 30,000 in North America. Emissions per package have come down too. Credit where it's due.

But here's the harder truth: Amazon's total emissions went up 16% last year. And for the first time since 2019, the company's pollution per dollar of revenue got worse, not better. A big part of that is the massive buildout of AI data centers, plus the sheer growth of the business.

Amazon can't fix all of that overnight. But last-mile delivery—the delivery vans that bring packages to our doors—is one place the company can turn things around right now. Amazon is already buying electric vehicles. What's missing is a commitment to finish the job: 100% clean deliveries by 2030. That's what we're asking for.

India is stepping up

Some exciting PMCA energy is coming from India. Last week, Indian Prime members started holding meetups in their cities and running a petition calling on Amazon to act. They started organizing in the middle of a brutal summer—record heatwaves, rising air pollution, cities measurably hotter than they were a decade ago. For many people in India, the problem is apparent at a glance. It's the air outside the window. Electrification of Amazon’s fleet would immediately have a positive impact on the air quality.

India is one of Amazon's fastest-growing markets, which means Indian customers have a powerful voice. If you're in India—or want to stand with members who are—you can sign and share the petition here.

We're working on the next meeting with Amazon

We've met with Amazon's sustainability team before, and now we're working to schedule a follow-up meeting in late August. These conversations are ongoing, so there aren’t major changes to report yet. That said, the fact that these meetings are happening at all is significant. Amazon rarely meets with customer groups…and only does when those customers matter. There are more than 30,000 of us, and we’re growing; that’s exactly why Amazon’s door stays open.

One thing you can do this week

Ask one person to join PMCA. That's it. If you know another Prime member—a friend, a coworker, someone in your family—tell them about Prime Members for a Cleaner Amazon and send them the website to join: primemembers.earth

Share the campaign with one person in your circle—on social media, by sharing this URL or simply texting the link primemembers.earth. With more than 30,000 of us on several continents, we have been able to get Amazon to speak directly to us. That doesn't happen for most customer campaigns. It's happening because we're not outsiders—we're the customers this company cares about. Let’s keep up the momentum

Amazon built its business on listening to customers like us. Right now, the company is listening. Let's make sure it can't stop.

Thank you for being a part of this. ✊

Krista, Roshan, Ogie, Michael, and William on behalf of the Prime Members for a Cleaner Amazon (PMCA) Leadership Team
