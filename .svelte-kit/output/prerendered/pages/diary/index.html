From your porch to Amazon's boardroom

The numbers are the evidence. The power is the people behind them. Amazon listens to its customers, so every log is also a signal that one more customer is paying attention and wants Amazon to do more.

  1. 1

    You log it

    Twenty seconds, from your porch or the sidewalk. A package that arrived in pieces, a box ten times too big, a gas van idling down your street.

  2. 2

    It adds up

    Your log joins everyone else's in the totals on this page. One porch is easy to ignore. Thousands of customers saying the same thing aren't.

  3. 3

    We take it to Amazon

    We use the totals to show Amazon, the press and the public the gap between Amazon's promises and what customers see, and to back our demands: electric vans, clean energy and better conditions for workers.

  4. 4

    Amazon has to answer

    The more people logging, the bigger the group Amazon has to answer to. That's how customers move a company that size: together, and with proof.

We show our working

Each figure says how many people logged and what it can and can't tell us. People choose what to log, so the totals show what customers saw, not how often it happens everywhere. Counts, never made-up percentages.

Your details stay yours

All we need is your ZIP or postal code. Your name and email are optional, and we only send you campaign updates if you tick the box to ask for them.

What stays private

  • No photos, and nothing from inside your Amazon account: no order numbers, no screenshots.
  • Location is only your postal code, or a postal code or town you type. Never a street address, never your phone's GPS.
  • Nothing location-level is shown publicly.
  • Never photograph, follow or report on delivery drivers. Most are contractors on monitored routes, and this campaign stands with them.
