Why join

A lot of us rely on Amazon and have mixed feelings about what that convenience costs. Last year, Amazon's climate pollution jumped 16%. And it's backing a huge new gas plant in Texas to power a data center.

Amazon has the money and technology to choose cleaner options. What will move it is us, its customers. When we organize and speak up together, we put at risk what Amazon protects most: our loyalty.

The holiday shopping season is Amazon's busiest time of year, when our voices carry furthest. That's why we're meeting now.

What we'll do together

  1. 1Hear from members

    PMCA members will share why they got involved and what happened when we took our concerns directly to Amazon.

  2. 2Start our Delivery Diary together

    We'll kick off the Delivery Diary holiday push, with everyone logging a first entry live. Together we'll build the record Amazon won't publish: how our packages actually arrive.

  3. 3Choose your next step

    We'll share what comes next and the ways you can step up, from signing our Texas gas plant petition to joining PMCA's leadership team.

You'll leave with one concrete thing to do this holiday season.

Who should come

Any Amazon customer, whether you're brand new or have been with us from the start. No experience needed.

If you can, have a recent Amazon delivery in mind. We'll log it together.

Hosted by Prime members across the US and Canada.
